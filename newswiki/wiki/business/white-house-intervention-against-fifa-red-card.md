# [White House Intervenes Behind the Scenes to Overturn FIFA Red Card Decision](https://cn.wsj.com/articles/white-house-fifa-balogun-red-card-world-cup-48c0c052?mod=cn_hp_lead_pos7)

Political interference in sports governance undermines public trust in international institutions.

## Key Points
- The U.S. government, through officials including Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and White House advisor Andrew Giuliani, initiated a legal and political campaign to challenge the red card against American forward Folarin Balogun.
- President Trump personally contacted FIFA President Gianni Infantino, urging a review of the red card decision, which was based on a VAR review of a potential foul involving a foot-on-foot contact.
- FIFA invoked its Article 27 discretionary authority to reverse the red card, citing a need for fair assessment and impartiality.
- The move sparked global backlash, with critics calling it a violation of sports integrity and a dangerous precedent for political interference in international sports governance.

## Key Stakeholders and Dynamics
- FIFA maintains that its disciplinary committee is independent, and the decision to reverse the red card was made internally by the committee, not through direct political pressure.
- Trump and Infantino have a long-standing relationship, with both appearing together at events such as UFC and peace summits, suggesting a history of political alignment.
- The U.S. Soccer Federation expressed concern over the lack of appeal rights and the absence of a suitable substitute player for Balogun.
- Belgian and Norwegian football officials condemned the reversal, with Belgian coach Rudi Garcia suggesting the incident may have been a 'hoax' and Norwegian coach Stale Solbakken calling it a 'very, very, very, very, very bad decision'.
