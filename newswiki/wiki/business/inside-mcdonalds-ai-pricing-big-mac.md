# [Inside McDonald’s push to have AI price your Big Mac](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/world/1656965/inside-mcdonald-s-push-to-have-ai-price-your-big-mac)

McDonald's is deploying AI-driven pricing across its franchise network to maximize corporate revenue, but the system widens price disparities, pressures franchisees, and invites antitrust scrutiny over algorithmic coordination.

## Key Points
- McDonald's uses a machine-learning pricing engine analyzing millions of daily transactions across ~14,000 US restaurants to generate "optimal price" recommendations per location per menu item, widening price gaps — e.g., a Big Mac costs $5.69 at one Fresno store and $6.89 (21% more) two miles away. [[business/mcdonalds-ai-pricing|McDonald's AI pricing engine]]
- Franchisees report pressure to follow AI recommendations despite McDonald's calling the portal "a tool, not a mandate"; internal communications require "constructively engaging" with approved pricing tools, and the company tracks "pricing non-compliance" in business reviews. [[business/franchise-model|Franchise model]] tensions
- McDonald's headquarters profits from a percentage of franchisee revenue, incentivizing lower prices to drive volume, while franchisees face 36% cost increases since 2019 and prefer higher prices to protect margins. [[business/franchise-economics|Franchise economics]]
- Antitrust regulators and courts are scrutinizing algorithmic pricing; McDonald's own portal terms warn franchisees they "may be competitors" and must comply with antitrust laws, acknowledging potential collusion risk. [[business/antitrust-algorithmic-pricing|Algorithmic pricing antitrust]]
- Tiger Analytics operates the AI platform under McDonald's rules: e.g., only raise prices on items not increased in 2+ years, only if 30%+ of stores recently raised them, and exclude ice cream/drinks from summer increases. [[tech/ai-pricing-platforms|AI pricing platforms]]
- US foot traffic has declined year-over-year every month since March 2026 despite conservative pricing pushes; CEO Kempczinski called out ~one-third of franchisees not complying with under-$3 menu guidance. [[business/mcdonalds-traffic-decline|McDonald's traffic trends]]
