# [The Skill Most Adults Never Learn | Simon Sinek](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RbyoqWrE66w)

Friendship is a neglected but essential life skill that demands the same intentional practice and prioritization we give to work and romantic relationships, because deep connections are the ultimate biohack for wellbeing, resilience, and leadership.

## Key Points
- Friendship is a skill that requires deliberate practice, yet there is an entire industry for leadership development and precious little for learning how to be a friend.
- Deep friendships are foundational: you struggle as a leader or partner without friends to call when things go sideways.
- Friendship acts as the ultimate biohack — it literally addresses rising anxiety, depression, and even longevity, yet few people are actually good at being friends.
- People readily cancel on friends for meetings but rarely cancel meetings for friends, revealing a systemic undervaluation of friendship compared to work and romantic relationships.
- Unlike work (coaches, conversations) or romantic relationships (counseling), friendships often end after one bad fight with no infrastructure for repair — no friendship therapists or coaches exist.
- Simon Sinek challenges us to audit who we spend time with: are they people who teach, inspire, celebrate, and create space for both our best and broken selves — or just warm bodies to avoid being alone?
