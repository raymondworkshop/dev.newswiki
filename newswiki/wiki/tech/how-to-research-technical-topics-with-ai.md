# [How to Research Technical Topics With AI](https://www.0xkato.xyz/how-to-research-technical-topics-with-ai/)

Durable technical understanding comes from narrowing questions, verifying sources independently, stress-testing explanations against objections, and reconstructing the topic from scratch before writing — using AI as a research accelerator, not an authority.

## Key Points
- Start by narrowing broad technical questions into scoped, ordered investigations with clear boundaries (e.g., "what happens after a prompt until next-token prediction" for LLMs).
- Use AI to identify missing terminology and dependency chains, then treat model outputs as leads — not sources — to verify against original papers, documentation, and code.
- Research one component at a time until you can explain its inputs, transformation, and outputs; stress-test your mental model by asking what a knowledgeable reader would object to.
- Keep exact technical claims and their provenance separate from the simplified explanations that make concepts click; record the limits of each simplification.
- Close the AI chat and reconstruct the topic from a blank page; gaps appear when you cannot explain the transition between adjacent components without the model's phrasing.
- Only then involve AI for prose: feed it structured notes, claim-checking records, and intended reader level — not the raw research chat with its dead ends and superseded guesses.
