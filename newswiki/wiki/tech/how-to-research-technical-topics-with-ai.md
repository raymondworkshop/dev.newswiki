# [How to Research Technical Topics With AI](https://www.0xkato.xyz/how-to-research-technical-topics-with-ai/)

Effective technical research with AI requires narrowing the question, iteratively validating explanations against primary sources, and reconstructing the knowledge without relying on the model.

## Key Points
- Narrow the original question to a well‑bounded sub‑problem (e.g., tokenization → embeddings → attention) before asking AI for help, ensuring the research stays focused and tractable.
- Use AI to generate ordered lists of concepts, locate terminology, and surface relevant sources, but always verify each claim against the primary source such as the original [[tech/transformer|Transformer]] paper or official documentation.
- Separate the raw technical claim from the simplified mental model; keep both side‑by‑side so that simplifications are not mistaken for literal implementations (e.g., [[hubs/attention-head|Attention Head]] projections).
- Iteratively challenge your explanations by seeking objections, testing edge cases, and reconstructing the topic from a blank page before drafting the final article.
