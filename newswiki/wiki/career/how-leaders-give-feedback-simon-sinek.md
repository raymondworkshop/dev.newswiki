# [How Leaders Give Feedback | Leading In Management | Simon Sinek](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lzpbeonJ-x0)

Simon Sinek argues that effective leaders give feedback by adapting to each person's personality, leading with empathy and care, and trusting their team first rather than demanding trust be earned.

## Key Points
- Reject "blunt honesty" — you can be honest without being degrading; adapt feedback style to the receiver's personality (some need more politeness, others can handle bluntness). [[hubs/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]]
- Good leadership starts with care and trust; leaders take the risk to trust first, not demand the team prove trustworthiness. [[career/leadership|Leadership]]
- View people as human beings first, not problems — they may have a problem, but they are not the problem. [[career/people-management|People Management]]
- Empathy transforms feedback: instead of threatening consequences for missed numbers, ask "are you okay? what's going on? I'm worried about you." [[career/empathy-at-work|Empathy at Work]]
