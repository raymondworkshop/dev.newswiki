# [Leadership's Unspoken Rules: Simon Sinek on Responsibility and Trust](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SHD9vKUSqGI)

True leadership means accepting that the perks of authority come with the obligation to protect your team first, earning loyalty through genuine care rather than rank alone.

## Key Points
- Leadership is not about being in charge; it is about taking care of those in your charge.
- Organizations operate on a deep-seated social contract: leaders receive perks (better pay, offices, parking) in exchange for the expectation that they will rush toward danger to protect the team when threats arise.
- The social contract breaks when leaders put themselves first — e.g., keeping high salaries during layoffs or missing arbitrary analyst projections while cutting jobs — which generates moral outrage.
- True leadership requires courage: accepting the perks of authority while fulfilling the responsibility to protect people, even at personal cost.
- Love and loyalty are earned when people feel seen, heard, understood, and that they matter as human beings — not from rank alone.
- [AI Synthesis] The framework reframes leadership perks not as privileges but as prepayment for the duty to absorb risk on behalf of the group.
