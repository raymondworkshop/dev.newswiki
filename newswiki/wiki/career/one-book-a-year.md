# [You should read at least one job-related book a year](https://will-keleher.com/posts/one-book-a-year/)

Reading at least one job-related book per year is a low-effort, high-return habit that compounds over time to meaningfully improve your career satisfaction and effectiveness.

## Key Points
- Being good at your job is intrinsically satisfying; quiet-quitting and mediocrity lead to dread and depression, while trying hard often feels easier in the long run.
- Most professionals do zero extracurricular learning for their jobs, yet even small, consistent efforts compound significantly over a career.
- Physical books are superior to videos or scaffolded courses for deep understanding — they demand the hard mental work that produces real learning.
- Specific technical books — *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, *High Performance MySQL*, *Effective TypeScript*, *The Scout Mindset*, *The Linux Command Line* — had outsized impact on the author's ability to solve hard problems and think clearly at work.
- The ROI is exceptional: ~$40 and a few evenings per book can meaningfully shift career trajectory and day-to-day effectiveness.
