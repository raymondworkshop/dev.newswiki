# [AI is removing the middle class of software engineering](https://blog.florianherrengt.com/ai-removing-middle-class-software-engineering.html)

AI pushes salaries further apart: high-judgment engineers become more valuable, while those who rely on AI as a substitute for understanding become expensive liabilities.

## Key Points
- AI has removed the 'speed limit' on producing low-quality code, allowing projects with weak engineering cultures to accumulate technical debt and fail significantly faster than before.
- Implementation has become a cheap commodity; the primary value of a software engineer has shifted from writing code to exercising technical judgment and managing system complexity.
- [AI Synthesis] The 'middle class' of engineering—those who primarily translate specifications into working code—is being hollowed out by [[hubs/LLM|LLMs]].
- Bad engineers are now a greater liability because they can generate massive amounts of 'slop' (e.g., 25,000-line PRs) that exceed the human capacity for review and remediation.
- Traditional productivity metrics like PR count and lines of code are now deceptive, as high output can actually steal productivity from the rest of the team through increased review burden and regressions.
