# [Simon Sinek on How to Make Better Choices and Live More Fully](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wij5NZpy6DE)

Simon Sinek argues that better decision-making comes from using personal filters aligned with purpose and passion rather than chasing money or ease, because pursuing what excites you ultimately leads to greater fulfillment and often greater success.

## Key Points
- Decision-making is a process defined by the filters you apply — financial reward, ease of work, or alignment with purpose and passion. [[career/_index|Career]]
- Sinek's college experience: choosing classes by low workload led to boredom; switching to professor ratings brought engagement and excitement to work hard. [[lifestyle/_index|Lifestyle]]
- Chasing only money creates a plateau — you eventually get bored or employers get bored of you; pursuing what excites you paradoxically often yields greater financial reward because you work harder with less strain. [[career/_index|Career]]
- Sinek's personal filters: "rather be happy than right, rather do good than get rich" — decisions should leave you feeling you contributed and enjoyed the experience, not just the payoff. [[lifestyle/_index|Lifestyle]]
- No judgment on others' filters; decision-making is personal — the key is keeping your own filters clear and consistent. [[career/_index|Career]]
