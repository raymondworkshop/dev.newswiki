# [Developing Taste](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/developing-taste)

Taste is a trainable instinct that becomes the decisive product differentiator in an AI-abundant world, developed by immersing in great work, rigorously analyzing why it works, and persistently practicing your craft through the taste gap.

## Key Points
- In a commoditized software landscape where AI makes functional products easy to build, taste — brand, design, intuitiveness, overall experience — becomes the key differentiator, as noted by Anu Atluru: "In a world of scarcity, we treasure tools. In a world of abundance, we treasure taste." [[design/_index|设计]]
- Good taste is a trained instinct, not mere personal preference; Steve Jobs advised exposing yourself to "the best things that humans have done" and bringing those standards into your own work, while Julie Zhuo recommends building a curated list of tastemakers to study. [[design/_index|设计]]
- Develop taste by analyzing why something feels great rather than relying on gut feeling: study interactions in apps (e.g., Brian Lovin's App Dissection), deconstruct directorial choices in film, and rationalize patterns behind great work. [[design/_index|设计]]
- Practice your craft and seek feedback; the gap between your taste and your output (the "taste gap" described by Ira Glass) is normal and signals your taste is already sharp enough to recognize the shortfall — persist through it to become a tastemaker. [[career/_index|职场]]
